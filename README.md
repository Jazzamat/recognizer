As Frank Herbert put in his famous novel, Dune, "The power to
destroy a thing is the absolute control over it" (Herbert, 1965). In a less dramatic tone, unless
we have the means to recognise something is AI, we can have no meaningful options as to
how we deal with it. So then, perhaps; “The power to recognise AI is the power to control it”.
